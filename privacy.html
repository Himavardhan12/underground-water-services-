Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: we only receive the details you choose to give us, such as your phone number, email and site location. We use them to answer your enquiry and carry out borewell work. We do not sell your information, and this website does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Information we collect

Sai Underground Water Services provides borewell drilling, borewell scanning and pumpset installation in Bengaluru. When you contact us through this website, by phone, by email or on WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address, if you provide them
  • Your site address, locality or plot details
  • Any photographs or documents you send about your site

This website has no accounts and no payment section. There is nothing to sign up for and no personal profile is created.

About the enquiry form: the form in the contact section runs in your browser. When you send it, your enquiry opens in WhatsApp with your details already filled in, and nothing leaves this website until you press send there. If WhatsApp does not open, call or email us instead.

How we use your information

We use what you tell us only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and answer your questions
  • Arrange a site visit or survey at a time that suits you
  • Provide an estimate and carry out drilling, scanning or pumpset work you agree to
  • Keep a record of work done and depths logged on your site
  • Meet legal and record-keeping obligations

We do not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we do not build advertising profiles from them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own. There is no advertising code, no remarketing pixel and no analytics script installed on this site.

Some third parties may receive basic technical information when your browser connects to them:

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on this site, so your browser requests those files directly from Google.
  • Google Maps and WhatsApp are linked from the contact section. Opening them takes you to those services, where their own privacy policies apply.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We share it only where it is needed to do the work you asked for, for example with our own crew or with a service provider helping us reach you, or where the law requires us to disclose it.

We keep enquiry details only for as long as they remain useful for the enquiry or the work carried out, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask us to.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time:

  • What information we hold about you
  • To correct anything that is wrong
  • To delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • To stop contacting you

Write to saihimavardhan001@gmail.com or call 95357 50539 and we will respond as soon as we can.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive documents you do not need to share.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when how the website or our services change. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last updated. Continued use of this website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
